Our Story

Built for Discerning Collectors

Established on Cambie Road in Richmond, British Columbia, ABCD Cars was founded on a simple conviction: the acquisition of an exotic automobile should be as extraordinary as the machine itself.

We deliberately rejected the high-volume, impersonal culture of traditional auto dealerships. Instead, we operate as a private automotive salon. Every vehicle in our climate-controlled gallery is personally vetted, rigorously authenticated, and maintained to museum-grade standards.

Whether you are acquiring a limited-production Porsche GT3, an Italian V12 flagship, or seeking bespoke allocation sourcing across Europe and North America, our private client advisors offer complete discretion, transparent pricing, and zero dealer documentation fees.

Integrity

The Four ABCD Hallmarks

Our standards exceed factory requirements to protect your investment and ensure unrivaled ownership peace of mind.

150-Point Certification

Electronic paint meter depth checks, laser chassis geometry scanning, dynamometer testing, and factory diagnostic scans to verify zero overrevs or hidden tracking.

Provenance Guarantee

We exclusively select vehicles with clean CARFAX histories, continuous authorized service logs, verified odometer telemetry, and clear unencumbered titles.

Climate Showroom Vault

All inventory is preserved within our 24/7 climate-controlled, air-filtered Richmond showroom, battery-tendered and detailed using Swissvax master products.

Enclosed Nationwide Delivery

Whether your residence is in West Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, or Montreal, our dedicated enclosed transport delivers your supercar directly to your garage.
