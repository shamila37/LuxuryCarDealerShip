Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about selling or consigning your exotic supercar with ABCD Cars.

How fast will I receive payment for a direct buyout?

Once vehicle inspection and title verification are completed, funds are wired immediately via Canadian certified bank wire or draft. Direct buyouts are routinely funded within 24 hours of vehicle intake.

Can you buy out my existing lease or bank lien?

Yes. As BC Licensed Dealer #40212, we communicate directly with your leasing company or financing bank (Porsche Financial, Ferrari Financial, BMW, etc.) to obtain official payout statements and settle liens in full.

Where is my car stored while on consignment?

All consigned vehicles are kept in our secured, 24/7 monitored, climate-controlled boutique showroom facility at 10740 Cambie Road, Richmond, BC. Vehicles are kept battery-tendered and never test-driven without vetted pre-qualification.

Do you accept vehicles located outside British Columbia?

Absolutely. We routinely acquire and consign vehicles across Alberta (Calgary, Edmonton), Ontario (Toronto, GTA), and Quebec. We coordinate fully insured enclosed vehicle transport right to our Richmond showroom.
